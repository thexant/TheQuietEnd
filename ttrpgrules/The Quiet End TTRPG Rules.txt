The Quiet End
A PbtA-based tabletop framework for roleplaying in “The Quiet End” universe
________________


1) Core Resolution
When you do something risky, roll 2d6 + Stat.
* 10+: You do it, cleanly. Take a bonus / avoid a cost.
* 7–9: You do it, but choose 1 cost / complication (or GM chooses).
* 6-: Mark XP; GM makes a move (hard as needed).
Stats:
* GRIT (endure, resist, push through)
* WITS (notice, plan, diagnose)
* TECH (repair, hack, operate systems)
* TACT (fight, command, pressure)
* FACE (negotiate, lie, read people)
________________


2) Harm, Stress, and Conditions
Keep it simple: two tracks.
2.1 Conditions
Each time you take damage or experience a situation where you may end up injured or otherwise hurt, physically or psychologically; add one of the following “Injury Tags”.
Pick from: Injured, Exposed, Shaken, Exhausted, Contaminated, Compromised
* If you’d take a Condition you already have, upgrade it to a Wound.
2.2 Wounds (serious)
* 2 Wounds = you’re out of the scene (Condition + Wound + Wound)
* 3 Wounds = Fill in 1 box on your Health Clock
The Health Clock is a box divided into 4. When all four boxes are filled, your character dies.
If you sustain 3 wounds and fill in a box on your health clock; each new wound of the same type immediately fills another box until you receive medical treatment/healing.
2.3 Radiation Track (setting-defining)
Each character has RADS 0–6.
* Gain RADS from Corridor exposure, bad shielding, contaminated salvage.
* At 4+ RADS, you’re unstable: you take -1 ongoing to resist exposure and healing is harder.
* At 6 RADS, you’re critical: immediate medical intervention or you’re done.
________________


3) The Crew Loop
Play cycles between four phases:
3.1 JOB PHASE (contract / objective play)
* Take a job (or self-declare a goal), gather intel, prep loadout, commit to a route.
* Play focuses on negotiation, planning, pressure, and the first consequences.
3.2 TRAVEL/CORRIDOR PHASE (structured travel)
* The ship makes a Corridor Run using Corridor Moves, events, and clocks.
* This is where instability, radiation, and route drift hit hardest.
3.3 LOCATION PHASE (on-station / on-world play)
* The crew is physically present somewhere: station, colony, outpost, freeport, derelict hub.
* Play focuses on: NPC interaction, side problems, favors, rumors, local politics, and recreation.
* This is where “truth is regional” shows up most: you hear things that are wrong, outdated, or dangerously true.

3.4 DOCKS PHASE (refit / recovery / projects)
   * Spend resources, run decon, repair, upgrade, and reduce Heat.
   * This phase is more “ship and character maintenance,” less “local drama.”

Important: Location Phase and Dock Phase often happen back-to-back, but they don’t have to. A place can be socially rich but mechanically hostile (no parts, no safe refit), or mechanically helpful but socially empty (a dead outpost with supplies but no people).
________________


4) GM Agenda & Principles
Agenda
      * Make the Corridors feel real, costly, and unreliable.
      * Keep the crew desperate but not helpless.
      * Make consequences delayed, regional, and political.
Principles
      * Ask questions; build on answers.
      * Put scarcity on-screen.
      * Show systems failing at the worst time.
      * Let rumors be true sometimes.
GM Moves (examples)
      * Advance an Instability / Radiation / Heat clock
      * Separate the crew (airlocks, comm failure, fog)
      * Offer a deal that solves one problem and creates another
      * Reveal the price of survival (people, ethics, time)
________________


5) Clocks (the engine of decay) - Expanded
5.0 What a Clock Is
A Clock is a simple countdown/progress tracker drawn as 4, 6, or 8 segments.
      * When you fill segments, the situation changes.
      * Clocks let you show pressure, decay, pursuit, scarcity, and long-term consequences without spreadsheets.
      * Clocks are public by default. If something is hidden, tell the table what the clock represents in vague terms (“Someone Is Coming”), but don’t reveal the details until it matters.

Clock sizes
         * 4 segments: fast, immediate problems (this scene / this phase)
         * 6 segments: medium pressure (this job / this travel leg)
         * 8 segments: slow burn (multi-session arcs, faction fallout, long projects)

________________


5.1 Types of Clocks You’ll Use
Use whichever fits the moment:
            1. Threat Clocks (bad things approaching)
Examples: Boarding Imminent, Quarantine Lockdown, Vacuum Bloom Spread, Gate uthority Sweep

            2. Decay Clocks (systems or situations wearing down)
Examples: Ship System Strain, Corridor Instability, Supply Famine
            3. Heat/Attention Clocks (people noticing you)
Examples: HEAT, Bounty Posted, Corp Audit, Pirate Interest
            4. Progress/Project Clocks (crew builds/earns something)
Examples: Install Decon Bay, Secure Fake Papers Network, Map a Stable Route
            5. Social Clocks (relationships shifting)
Examples: Contact Loyalty, Union Trust, Captain’s Reputation

________________


5.2 When to Start a Clock
Start a clock when a situation is not solved by a single roll and needs pressure over time.
Good prompts:
               * “This is going to take a while…”
               * “Even if you succeed, it’ll cost time or wear something down…”
               * “Someone will react to this, but not immediately…”
               * “You’re safe for now…”

Rule of thumb: If you expect 2+ scenes of consequences, make a clock.
________________


5.3 How Clocks Advance
Clocks tick in three main ways:
A) GM Moves
 Whenever you make a GM move that escalates the situation, tick a relevant clock.
B) Player Costs (7–9 results)
 When players accept complications (lose time, draw attention, strain systems), that’s usually +1 segment somewhere.
C) Phase Triggers
 Use the game’s phase loop to drive ticking:
                  * Job Phase: clocks start (contracts, rivals, deadlines); Heat often rises
                  * Travel/Corridor Phase: Instability + Strain spikes; hazards tick clocks fast
                  * Location Phase: social clocks shift; rumors mutate; local threats activate
                  * Docks Phase: clocks can be reduced/cleared via repairs, decon, bribes, restock

How many segments to add
                     * +1 for minor complication / time cost / small attention

                     * +2 for major failure / serious damage / loud exposure / hard escalation
                     * +3 only for catastrophic events (collapse, breach, mass panic)

________________


5.4 What Happens When a Clock Fills
When a clock is filled, something changes immediately. Don’t just say “it fills”; make it bite.
                        * Threat clock fills: the threat arrives or triggers
                        * Decay clock fills: the system fails / breaks / becomes unavailable
                        * Heat clock fills: enforcement/pirates/corps take action
                        * Project clock fills: the upgrade/service/network is complete
                        * Social clock fills: trust is secured OR the relationship snaps

After a clock fills, either:
                           * Reset it (new cycle, higher stakes), or
                           * Replace it (the situation has changed form)

________________


5.5 Core Clocks
A) CORRIDOR INSTABILITY (per route or per region)
Type: Decay / Threat (6 segments recommended)
Represents: How close a Corridor is to a Shift or Collapse event.
Tick it when:
                              * A Corridor Run gets complications (fog, radiation surge, route drift)
                              * You “lose time” during travel or miss the alignment window
                              * You use an ungated exit / emergency maneuver
                              * You push a gate window beyond safe tolerances

Threshold effects (example):
                                 * 2/6: Warning signs (odd readings, “sour” alignment, contradictory nav data)
                                 * 4/6: Shift event likely soon (route options narrow; gate realignment pressure)
                                 * 6/6: Trigger a major event: Shift or Collapse (GM choice, or roll if you want uncertainty)

Optional rule (adds nice texture):
 When it hits 6/6, roll 1d6:
                                    * 1–3: Shift
                                    * 4–5: Violent Shift (misexit + system strain)
                                    * 6: Collapse

(If you prefer pure narrative control, skip the roll.)
________________


B) SHIP SYSTEM STRAIN (ship-wide wear)
Type: Decay (6 segments recommended)
Represents: The ship accumulating hidden problems: microfractures, fouled filters, EMI scars, jury-rigs nearing failure.
Tick it when:
                                       * You take a “works but costs” repair result
                                       * You run the corridor under stress (fog, radiation, partial shielding)
                                       * You go too long without proper Docks maintenance
                                       * You patch instead of properly fixing

Threshold effects (example):
                                          * 2/6: A system becomes Strained (GM picks, or tie it to the fiction)
                                          * 4/6: Another system becomes Strained or one becomes Damaged
                                          * 6/6: A system goes Offline at the worst moment, or HULL takes a hit

Clearing it:
                                             * Docks Phase ship actions (proper refit, spending PARTS, decon, calibration)
                                             * Some modules reduce how often it ticks (Fog-Hardened Controls, Decon Bay, etc.)

________________


C) HEAT (attention and consequences)
Type: Attention (4 or 6 segments depending on tone)
Represents: How “known” and “wanted” the crew/ship is in the current region.
Tick it when:
                                                * You act loud, violent, or illegal in public
                                                * You double-cross someone important
                                                * You fail social moves that create suspicion
                                                * You arrive during lockdown/quarantine and don’t comply

Threshold effects (example):
                                                   * 2/6: You get tagged (ID checks, tailing, denied docking, price hikes)
                                                   * 4/6: Active pursuit (raids, warrants, pirate ambush setup, corp bounty)
                                                   * 6/6: A major response (ship impounded, contact flips, gate authority hunt)

Clearing it:
                                                      * “Keep It Quiet” results, bribes, favors, forged papers, laying low
                                                      * Leaving the region can reduce Heat, but it may convert into a long-term bounty clock

________________


D) SUPPLY FAMINE (life support scarcity)
Type: Decay (6 segments recommended)
Represents: Food/meds/filters/air reserves becoming the crew’s real enemy.
Tick it when:
                                                         * Time passes without resupply
                                                         * You take “lose supplies” complications
                                                         * Systems strain causes inefficiency (leaks, fouled filters, decon failures)
                                                         * You take on extra passengers / refugees / “temporary crew”

Threshold effects (example):
                                                            * 2/6: Rationing (minor penalties, morale strain, Conditions appear easier)
                                                            * 4/6: Hard choices (trade gear for food, deny aid, risk contamination)
                                                            * 6/6: Crisis (someone gets sick, life support fails, mutiny pressure, desperate job)

Clearing it:
                                                               * Restock at docks, trade cargo, favors with stations/colonies, “good” salvage

________________


E) RUMOR WARP (truth decays as it travels)
Type: Social/Information Decay (4 or 6 segments)
Represents: The setting’s no-FTL reality: your info gets stale, distorted, weaponized.
Tick it when:
                                                                  * You rely on old DATA to make plans
                                                                  * You spend long time in transit
                                                                  * You spread a story publicly (especially if it benefits you)
                                                                  * A faction deliberately muddies the waters

Threshold effects (example):
                                                                     * 2/6: Minor distortions (prices differ, names wrong, dates off)
                                                                     * 4/6: Dangerous misinformation (wrong gate window, fake quarantine, “safe route” trap)
                                                                     * 6/6: Local myth hardens into policy (someone acts on the false truth; you pay)

Clearing it:
                                                                        * Fresh intel (Work the Crowd), verified nav data, trusted contacts, physical records
(Or embrace it: weaponize rumor as a tactic.)

________________


5.6 Quick Table Rule (How to Use Clocks Without Slowing Play)
If you want a fast default:
                                                                           * On any 7–9 where the cost is time/strain/attention → +1 segment somewhere.
                                                                           * On any 6- → +2 segments to the most relevant clock, or +1 to two clocks.
                                                                           * At end of each Phase → tick any clock that logically worsens if nothing was done.

6) Playbooks and Gear (character-facing rules)
6.0 Character Creation (quick)
                                                                           1. Pick a Playbook (below).
                                                                           2. Choose a Stats Array from your playbook.
                                                                           3. Pick 2 Playbook Moves (you start with those).
                                                                           4. Pick a Load (Light / Standard / Heavy) and choose your items.
                                                                           5. Choose 1 Mod (optional at start; set to 0–1 depending on tone).
                                                                           6. Create 2 Bonds (history questions) with other PCs.

6.1 Playbooks
The Navigator
You keep the crew alive between stars. You read the Corridors like the weather.
Recommended Stat Build: 

WITS +2, TECH +1, FACE +1, GRIT 0, TACT −1

You start with: Nav-slate (Precise), dosimeter (Precise), route charm or ritual token (Quiet)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                              * Sense the Sour: When you Read the Situation about a route, gate, or Corridor, on a hit ask +1 extra question. On a 10+, also gain +1 DATA.

                                                                              * Stitch the Window: Once per Corridor Run, you may turn a 7–9 on RUN A GATED CORRIDOR into a 10+. If you do, mark Exhausted or tick SHIP SYSTEM STRAIN +1.

                                                                              * Dead Reckoning: When you REALIGN THE GATE WINDOW, on a 10+ you may also reduce CORRIDOR INSTABILITY by 1 segment (if it makes sense).

                                                                              * Starblind: When sensors fail (fog/EMI), you can still navigate. When the crew would lose time from blindness, reduce that cost (GM turns “lose time” into a different cost).

                                                                              * Misexit Expert: When you exit “wrong side” of a system, you can declare you know a shortcut. Roll +WITS: 10+ you save a day; 7–9 you save hours but spend 1 FUEL or take 1 Heat.

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                 * I’ve seen ___ keep it together when space tried to kill us.
                                                                                 * ___ doesn’t respect the Corridor. That will get them hurt.
                                                                                 * I owe ___ for dragging me back from a bad run.

The Engineer
You keep the ship running with spit, wire, and stubbornness.
Recommended Stat Build:
TECH +2, GRIT +1, WITS +1, TACT 0, FACE −1

You start with: Tool harness (Rugged), patch-foam canister (Rugged, Limited), insulated gloves (Insulated)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                                    * Jury-Rig Miracle: When you DO THE FIX without Parts, you can still make it work. On a hit, it works—but the system gains a hidden flaw (GM clocks it) and SHIP SYSTEM STRAIN ticks +1.

                                                                                    * Hard Reset: When you take a quiet moment to rebuild a failing subsystem, spend 1 PARTS to clear Strained on one ship system without a roll.

                                                                                    * Overbuild: When you PATCH THE HULL, on a 10+ also choose one: regain +1 PARTS (from salvage/scrap) or reduce future Hull-related complications on this job.

                                                                                    * EM-Hardened Instinct: When Static Fog hits, you and your gear ignore the first “systems go blind” consequence (you still feel it—just not as badly).

                                                                                    * Spare the Good Stuff: When a result would cost Parts, you may instead cost time. Tick CORRIDOR INSTABILITY +1 (window tightens) or take +1 Heat—your choice.

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                       * I’ve patched ___ up more times than they know.
                                                                                       * ___ is the only one I trust near the reactor.
                                                                                       * I don’t forgive ___ for what they did to my ship.

The Medic
You keep bodies functional in places bodies were never meant to go.
Recommended Stat Build:
TECH +2, WITS +1, FACE +1, GRIT 0, TACT −1



You start with: Medkit (Medical, Limited), sealant bandages (Medical, Limited), respirator (Sealed)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                                          * Stitch & Stabilize: When you treat someone in the field, roll +TECH. 10+: clear a Condition or downgrade a Wound to a Condition. 7–9: you do it, but spend a charge (Limited) or take time.

                                                                                          * Rad Protocols: When you run decon or enforce shielding discipline, you can reduce RADS gain: the first time each session someone would gain RADS from travel/exposure, reduce it by 1 (min 0).

                                                                                          * Quarantine Voice: When you PUT PRESSURE ON to make people follow safety procedure, roll +FACE instead of +TACT.

                                                                                          * Know What’s Wrong: When you Read the Situation about illness/contamination, on a hit ask +1 extra question and the GM must answer truthfully.

                                                                                          * Pain Management: When someone would be taken out of a scene by Wounds, you can keep them in for a little longer. They stay, but take Exhausted and you start a 4-clock: Crash.

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                             * I’ve seen what ___ will become if they keep taking RADS like that.
                                                                                             * ___ saved my life. I won’t forget it.
                                                                                             * I don’t trust ___ with medical decisions.

The Quartermaster
You keep the ship fed, fueled, and profitable—and you know what to hide.
Recommended Stat Build:
FACE +2, WITS +1, TECH +1, GRIT 0, TACT −1

You start with: manifest slate (Precise), lockbox (Rugged), utility drone (Bulky) or drone-control band (Precise)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                                                * Balance the Books: Once per phase, you may convert one ship resource into another (1-for-1) if it makes sense in the fiction (trade/salvage/favors). The GM adds a string: +1 Heat, a debt, or a rumor complication.

                                                                                                * Drone Wrangler: When you use drones to scout/salvage/repair, roll +TECH. 10+: gain +1 PARTS or +1 DATA and avoid danger. 7–9: gain it, but the drone is damaged, lost, or brings trouble back.

                                                                                                * Ghost Manifest: When you try to move restricted cargo or fake paperwork, roll +FACE. 10+: it passes. 7–9: it passes but costs (Heat, a bribe, or you owe someone). 6-: it passes… and gets noticed later (start a clock).

                                                                                                * Stockpiler: When you SCAVENGE THE DEAD, on a hit gain +1 extra resource (your pick) but it’s tagged: Contaminated or Illegal (GM chooses).

                                                                                                * Knows Everyone: When you MAKE A CONTACT, on a 7–9 you may take the “solid” result anyway—but the contact also has a personal demand.

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                                   * ___ doesn’t understand what scarcity means. I do.
                                                                                                   * I’ve covered for ___—and they owe me for it.
                                                                                                   * ___ makes my deals harder than they need to be.

The Captain
You make the calls people hate—and you carry the consequences.
Recommended Stat Build:
FACE +2, GRIT +1, TACT +1, WITS 0, TECH −1

You start with: command token/authority papers (Precise), sidearm (Rugged), comms earpiece (Quiet)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                                                      * Call the Shot: When you give an order that’s actually actionable, roll +FACE. 10+: an ally takes +1 forward and avoids a cost. 7–9: +1 forward, but you take Heat or the ship takes Strain.
                                                                                                      * Hold the Line: When panic, mutiny, or despair hits, roll +GRIT. 10+: everyone steadies; clear Shaken on one person. 7–9: it holds, but you owe someone or sacrifice a resource.
                                                                                                      * The Offer: When you MAKE THE DEAL, on a 10+ you also choose one: reduce Heat by 1, gain +1 DATA, or secure a future favor.
                                                                                                      * We Don’t Leave People: When you choose to rescue instead of profit, mark XP and take +1 ongoing to related rolls for the rest of the job. The GM will absolutely make it cost you.
                                                                                                      * Bad Reputation: When you enter a place where your name matters, declare what they’ve heard about you. Take +1 forward when acting in line with that reputation, but the GM gets a free complication.

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                                         * ___ thinks I’m wrong about what we did. They’re not wrong to think that.
                                                                                                         * ___ is the reason this crew hasn’t fallen apart.
                                                                                                         * I’ve promised ___ I’ll get them home. Somehow.

The Security Officer
You keep people in line, keep boards from happening, and make sure the ship isn’t “easy prey.”
Recommended Stat Build:

TACT +2, GRIT +1, WITS +1, TECH 0, FACE −1

You start with: armor vest (Rugged, Bulky), stun baton (Rugged, Quiet), restraint zipcuffs (Rugged, Limited)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                                                         * Boarding Protocol: When you Put Pressure On during a boarding/raid/inspection, on a hit you also choose 1: hold them at range, protect a system from damage, or prevent a hostage situation.

                                                                                                         * Clear the Passage: When you force a route through danger (crowd, riot, tight corridor, zero-G), roll +TACT. 10+: you get through cleanly. 7–9: you get through but someone takes a Condition or you leave something behind.

                                                                                                         * Rules of Engagement: Once per scene you can reduce physical harm to you or an ally by 1. If you do, either mark Exhausted or tick HEAT +1 (it was loud or visible).

                                                                                                         * Security Sweep: When you arrive somewhere, you may immediately Read the Situation about threats here without rolling. The GM still answers, but also introduces a “security complication” you’ve noticed.

                                                                                                         * Intimidation Is a Tool: When you Make the Deal by threat instead of charm, roll +TACT instead of +FACE.

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                                            * ___ is the one person I won’t put hands on, no matter what.

                                                                                                            * ___ treats violence like a game. That’s a problem.

                                                                                                            * I owe ___ for stopping me from going too far.

The Broker
You don’t fix engines, you fix problems. Contacts, papers, favors, black-market logistics.

Recommended Stat Build:
FACE +2, WITS +1, GRIT +1, TECH 0, TACT −1

You start with: forged papers packet (Illegal, Precise), encrypted slate (Precise), bribe chits (Quiet, Limited)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                                                               * I Know a Person: Once per Location Phase, declare a plausible contact here. Roll +FACE. On a 10+: they’re available and helpful. 7–9: helpful, but they want payment now or they’re trouble-adjacent.

                                                                                                               * Cutouts: When you would gain Heat from a deal, you can route it through someone else. Reduce Heat by 1, but start a 4-clock: Loose End. When the clock fills, the next heat level gained is directed to you/the party and +1 if from the same source.

                                                                                                               * Dirty Luxury: When you Indulge, on a hit you may also reduce HEAT by 1 or gain +1 DATA (your choice). On a 7–9, you also pick up a small debt.

                                                                                                               * Paper Shield: When authorities inspect you, roll +FACE. On a 10+: they pass you through. 7–9: they pass you through, but take a resource (SUPPLY/PARTS/DATA) or give you a condition: Compromised.

                                                                                                               * Side Deal: Once per Job, when someone else makes a deal, you can “improve the terms.” Roll +FACE. On a hit, add a benefit. On a 7–9, also add a hidden complication (GM starts a clock).

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                                                  * ___ thinks I’m immoral. They’re not wrong, they’re just inexperienced.

                                                                                                                  * I’ve promised ___ I’d never sell them out.

                                                                                                                  * ___ is useful — which makes them a target.

The Reclaimer
Derelicts, ghost colonies, dead stations — you go where the profit still has teeth.
Recommended Stat Build:
WITS +2, TECH +1, GRIT +1, TACT 0, FACE −1

You start with: cutting torch (Cutting, Bulky), vac-suit shell (Sealed, Bulky), dosimeter (Precise)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                                                                     * Salvage Instinct: When you Scavenge the Dead, on a hit gain +1 extra resource. If you take the bonus, the GM tags it Contaminated or Illegal.

                                                                                                                     * Bloom Sense: When Vacuum Bloom is present, you always get a warning sign before it becomes lethal. The first Bloom consequence each session is reduced in severity.

                                                                                                                     * Zero-G Ghost: When moving through derelicts/void, roll +WITS instead of +GRIT to Get Through It.

                                                                                                                     * Strip It Clean: During Docks, you may convert 1 cargo/derelict access into +2 PARTS. If you do, tick SHIP SYSTEM STRAIN +1 (you pushed tools/crew too hard) or HEAT +1 (someone noticed).

                                                                                                                     * Black Box Recovery: When you recover logs/records from a wreck, gain +1 DATA and ask the GM one direct question about what happened here. On a 7–9, the answer is true but incomplete.

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                                                        * ___ is too soft for salvage work. I keep them alive anyway.

                                                                                                                        * ___ once left something behind in a wreck. I remember.

                                                                                                                        * I trust ___ to watch my back in the dark.

The Cipher
You don’t chart stars — you validate truth, crack systems, scrub trails, and turn raw signals into decisions.
Recommended Stat Build:
WITS +2, TECH +1, FACE +1, GRIT 0, TACT −1

You start with: encrypted slate (Precise), signal tracer (Precise), intrusion spikes (Precise, Limited)
Choose 2 Moves:
                                                                                                                           * Chain of Custody: When you gain DATA from salvage, rumors, or a contact, you may immediately roll +WITS to verify it.

                                                                                                                              * 10+: it’s clean—also reduce RUMOR WARP by 1 segment (if relevant).

                                                                                                                              * 7–9: it’s mostly true, but pick 1: late, partial, or planted.

                                                                                                                              * 6-: it’s compromised—GM starts a clock tied to who wants you misled.

                                                                                                                                 * Black Box Forensics: When you recover logs from a wreck/site, gain +1 DATA and ask the GM one direct question about what happened here. On a 7–9, the answer is true but creates a new problem (quarantine flag, Heat, contamination risk, or a pursuer clock).

                                                                                                                                 * Spoof & Scrub: When you try to avoid being tracked (IDs, transponders, docking logs), roll +TECH.

                                                                                                                                    * 10+: reduce HEAT by 2 or prevent it from increasing this scene.

                                                                                                                                    * 7–9: reduce HEAT by 1, but spend 1 DATA or 1 PARTS (your choice).

                                                                                                                                    * 6-: it “works”… and leaves a signature (start a 4-clock: Trace).

                                                                                                                                       * Signal Ghost: When systems go blind (Static Fog / EMI / sensor crash), you can keep one channel alive. Choose one: Comms, Sensors, or Nav telemetry. The crew avoids the first complication tied to that channel this session.

                                                                                                                                       * Information Warfare: When you deliberately spread or suppress a story to shape a situation, roll +WITS (planning) or +FACE (performance)—your choice.

                                                                                                                                          * 10+: pick 2. 7–9: pick 1.
Options: create a distraction, sour a rival’s deal, draw someone out, reduce Heat by 1, gain +1 DATA.

Bond prompts (choose 2):
                                                                                                                                             * ___ keeps acting on gut feelings; I keep catching the hidden costs.

                                                                                                                                             * I know something about ___ that I haven’t said out loud.

                                                                                                                                             * ___ saved me from a bad trace once; I still owe them.


6.2 Load and Starting Items
Load

                                                                                                                                                * Light (3 items): quick, quiet, mobile
                                                                                                                                                * Standard (5 items): prepared
                                                                                                                                                * Heavy (7 items + Bulky): armored / tool-heavy; GM can lean harder on “lose time / draw attention”

Rule: If you carry something Bulky, it counts as 2 items unless you’re Heavy load.

6.4 Mods & Augments
A Mod gives a benefit tag + a drawback tag.

Below are some examples, though GMs and players can create their own using the following as a guideline.
                                                                                                                                                                  * Subdermal Plating: Armored (reduce physical harm by 1), drawback: Magnetic Signature (easier to track)

                                                                                                                                                                  * Neural Jack: Precise (take +1 forward when interfacing with ship systems), drawback: Feedback (on a 6-, take Shaken or +1 RADS—your call)
                                                                                                                                                                  * Muscle Weave: Rugged (ignore first “Exhausted” you’d take), drawback: Metabolic Drain (tick SUPPLY FAMINE +1 when you push it)

8) Basic Moves (everyone has these)
GET THROUGH IT (endure danger) - roll +GRIT
10+: hold steady; choose 2.
7–9: choose 1 and GM chooses 1.
Choices: avoid harm, keep gear intact, don’t lose time, don’t gain RADS.
READ THE SITUATION - roll +WITS
Ask 2 (10+) or 1 (7–9):
                                                                                                                                                                     * What’s the real threat here?
                                                                                                                                                                     * What’s about to fail?
                                                                                                                                                                     * Who has leverage?
                                                                                                                                                                     * What’s being hidden / missing?
                                                                                                                                                                     * What’s my best way in/out?
DO THE FIX - roll +TECH
On a hit, stabilize a system or repair something.
                                                                                                                                                                     * 10+: also clear “Strained” or regain +1 PARTS (your call).
                                                                                                                                                                     * 7–9: it works but costs: time, Parts, or creates a new flaw.
PUT PRESSURE ON - roll +TACT
On a hit, you gain advantage in a standoff/fight.
                                                                                                                                                                     * 10+: pick 2. 7–9: pick 1.
Options: disarm, take position, force retreat, protect someone, capture.
MAKE THE DEAL - roll +FACE
                                                                                                                                                                        * 10+: favorable terms + useful info
                                                                                                                                                                        * 7–9: fair terms or good info (pick one)
                                                                                                                                                                        * 6-: you still get something, but you owe, get tagged, or Heat spikes

9) Ship Moves (Ship Stat + Operator)
9.1 How Ship Rolls Work (the “Operator” rule)
Whenever you trigger a Ship Move:
                                                                                                                                                                        1. Pick an Operator (the PC actually doing it at the console / in the suit / calling the play).
                                                                                                                                                                        2. Roll 2d6 + Ship Stat (the stat named by the move).
                                                                                                                                                                        3. Apply Operator Edge (based on the Operator’s relevant character stat):

Operator Edge
                                                                                                                                                                           * If the Operator’s relevant stat is +2 → +1 to the roll
                                                                                                                                                                           * If it’s +1 or 0 → +0
                                                                                                                                                                           * If it’s −1 → −1 to the roll

This keeps Ship Moves ship-forward, but still rewards having the right person doing the job.
Help / Crew Assist (optional but clean):
 One PC can assist if they’re meaningfully involved. They either:
                                                                                                                                                                              * Spend 1 SUPPLY or take Exhausted, and the Operator takes +1 forward on the roll; or
                                                                                                                                                                              * If they have a relevant tool/module/tag in the fiction, they can give +1 forward with no cost (GM may still tick a clock on a 7–9 result).

System State Penalties (fast + brutal):
 If the ship system tied to the move is:
                                                                                                                                                                                 * Strained: take −1
                                                                                                                                                                                 * Damaged: take −2
                                                                                                                                                                                 * Offline: you can’t roll it normally—you must first restore a workaround (GM says what that looks like), or you’re making a desperate move with a nasty cost.

RUN A GATED CORRIDOR — roll +SHIELDS
Trigger: You enter a Corridor that’s stabilized/held open by active Gates


Operator stat: WITS (nav/reads) or TECH (systems handling)
                                                                                                                                                                                    * 10+ Choose 2 Benefits.
                                                                                                                                                                                    * 7–9 Choose 1 Benefit; GM adds 1 Complication.
                                                                                                                                                                                    * 6− GM adds 2 Complications, and advance CORRIDOR INSTABILITY.
Benefits (choose):
                                                                                                                                                                                    * Clean Window: No one gains RADS from transit, and Shielding does not become Strained.
                                                                                                                                                                                    * On-Target Exit: No route drift / you arrive where you meant to (no “wrong side” arrival).
                                                                                                                                                                                    * Fast Passage: Save time or save 1 FUEL (your choice).
                                                                                                                                                                                    * Quiet Through the Gate: Prevent HEAT from increasing due to traffic/authority this leg.

Complications (GM picks or roll Gated Events):
                                                                                                                                                                                       * Use the Gated Corridor Events list (below). Gate authority complications are valid here.
________________


RUN AN UNGATED CORRIDOR — roll +SHIELDS
Trigger: You commit to an Ungated Corridor segment (no Gate window holding it stable).
Operator stat: WITS or TECH


On a hit (7+), you make it through—but Ungated corridors always exact a price.
                                                                                                                                                                                       * 10+ Choose 2 Benefits and 1 Cost.
                                                                                                                                                                                       *  7–9 Choose 1 Benefit and 2 Costs; GM adds 1 Complication.
                                                                                                                                                                                       *  6− GM adds 2 Complications, choose 3 Costs, and advance CORRIDOR INSTABILITY +2.


REALIGN THE GATE WINDOW — roll +SENSORS
Trigger: A Corridor Shift hits and you’re chasing the new coordinates / alignment.
Operator stat: WITS (pattern-reading) or TECH (calibration).
                                                                                                                                                                                       * 10+ You lock it in quickly; gain +1 DATA (“Found the Thread”) or reduce CORRIDOR INSTABILITY by 1 segment (if it makes sense).
                                                                                                                                                                                       * 7–9 You lock it in, but spend 1 PARTS or take +1 HEAT.
                                                                                                                                                                                       * 6− You misread it: the exit point is worse than expected (GM makes it bite: misexit, time loss, strain, exposure, pursuit, etc.).
Benefits (choose):
                                                                                                                                                                                       * Found the Thread: Gain +1 DATA (usable nav truth, hard readings, verified drift pattern).
                                                                                                                                                                                       * Tight Enough: You avoid route drift (arrive close/on-target).
                                                                                                                                                                                       * Shield Bubble: No one gains RADS from transit (still pay Costs as normal).
                                                                                                                                                                                       * Slip the Dark: Reduce HEAT by 1 or avoid being followed/marked this leg (your choice).

Costs (pick the required number):
                                                                                                                                                                                          * Spend 1 FUEL (course correction / holding the thread).
                                                                                                                                                                                          * Tick SHIP SYSTEM STRAIN +1 (shield cycling, fouled filters, EMI scarring).
                                                                                                                                                                                          * Someone takes Exposed or gains +1 RADS (your choice, fiction decides who).
                                                                                                                                                                                          * Lose time (miss a clean alignment / tight schedule): tick CORRIDOR INSTABILITY +1.

Complications (GM picks or roll Ungated Events):
                                                                                                                                                                                             * Use the Ungated Corridor Events list (below). Gate authority complications generally do not apply here.

PATCH THE HULL — roll +HULL (spend 1 PARTS)
Trigger: The ship takes damage, breaches, microfractures, or emergency structural failure.
Operator stat: TECH (repair) or GRIT (brute survival patching).
                                                                                                                                                                                             * 10+ Patch holds: repair damage and clear Strained on the relevant ship system/section (if appropriate).
                                                                                                                                                                                             * 7–9 Patch holds, but choose 1:
                                                                                                                                                                                             * It’s temporary (will fail at the worst time; GM starts/advances a clock)
                                                                                                                                                                                             * It takes hours (lose time / miss windows)
                                                                                                                                                                                             * It reduces HULL max until proper refit
                                                                                                                                                                                             * 6− You stop the worst of it… but the ship pays: lose extra PARTS, take system damage, depressurization, or someone gets tagged (Exposed/Contaminated/etc.).

SCAVENGE THE DEAD — roll +SENSORS (or +DRIVE if you’re doing a risky approach)
Trigger: You salvage a wreck/derelict while it’s still dangerous (radiation, fog, vacuum bloom, pirates, time pressure).
Operator stat: WITS (spot value / read risk) or TECH (drones / cutting / access).
                                                                                                                                                                                             * 10+ Gain 2: (SUPPLY / PARTS / FUEL / DATA).
                                                                                                                                                                                             *  7–9 Gain 1, but choose 1 cost:
                                                                                                                                                                                             * It’s Contaminated (GM tags it; RADS risk, decon required)
                                                                                                                                                                                             * It’s Illegal (mark/advance HEAT, or start a “Loose End” clock)
                                                                                                                                                                                             * It takes too long (lose time / advance a travel/pressure clock)
                                                                                                                                                                                             * You drew attention (advance HEAT / pursuer clock)
                                                                                                                                                                                             * 6− You find something—and it finds you (hazard triggers, boarders, bloom, quarantine ping, drone loss, etc.). Gain 1 anyway, but the GM hits hard.
(Your playbooks already hook into this move; this version just anchors it to ship capability first.)

KEEP IT QUIET — roll +SENSORS or +DRIVE (choose an approach)
Trigger: You try to avoid attention: spoof logs, kill signatures, slide through inspection windows, or vanish into noise.
Pick one approach:
                                                                                                                                                                                             * Ghost the signal (spoof transponder, scrub docking records, jam traces): roll +SENSORS
                                                                                                                                                                                             * Run dark (maneuver, hide in traffic/fog, silent drift burns): roll +DRIVE

Operator stat: FACE (cover story / handling authority), TECH (spoofing), or WITS (timing/reads)—pick what matches the approach.
                                                                                                                                                                                                * 10+ Choose 2:
                                                                                                                                                                                                * Reduce HEAT by 2 (or prevent it from increasing this scene)
                                                                                                                                                                                                * You avoid inspection / tracking entirely
                                                                                                                                                                                                * You create a false trail (GM starts a clock on someone else)
                                                                                                                                                                                                * You keep cargo/people hidden (no extra complications)
                                                                                                                                                                                                * 7–9 Choose 1, but the GM adds 1:
                                                                                                                                                                                                * You had to pay: spend 1 (SUPPLY or DATA or PARTS)
Someone clocks something: start/advance a Trace / Audit / Loose End clock
It works… but only until the next major stop (a delayed consequence)
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * 6− It “works,” but leaves a signature: start a 4-clock: TRACE, and the GM picks the first tick immediately.
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * No one gains RADS
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * You learn a rumor-map (gain +1 DATA)
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Shielding strains (system becomes Strained/Damaged)
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Someone takes Exposed or +RADS
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Route drift: exit far from intended point (lose time / Fuel)
                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Unwelcome arrival: pirates, gate authority, quarantine

11) Location Phase and Dock Phase
11.1 Location Phase Structure
When you arrive somewhere with people (or meaningful traces of them), do the following:
                                                                                                                                                                                                   1. Establish the Local Truth

                                                                                                                                                                                                      * GM states what everyone believes here about the region (may be wrong).
                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Add 1–2 Local Tags to the location (examples below).

                                                                                                                                                                                                         2. Set the Pressure
 Pick (or roll) one:

                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Someone wants something from you right now
                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Your arrival creates attention (Heat or suspicion)
                                                                                                                                                                                                            * A local problem is about to become your problem
                                                                                                                                                                                                            * You have a narrow window before the Corridor shifts again

                                                                                                                                                                                                               3. Each PC takes 1–2 Location Actions
 These are short scenes. Not everything needs a roll—roll when it’s risky or consequential.

                                                                                                                                                                                                               4. Resolve Fallout

                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Advance or clear clocks.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Record new debts/favors.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Update Heat, Data, Supplies.

Local Tags (examples):
                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Company-Tight, Union-Run, Quarantine, Hungry, Flush-With-Parts, Cultish, Pirate-Leaning, No-Questions-Asked, Truth-Blackout, Gate-Authority, Radiation-Sick, Refugee-Swollen

________________


11.2 Location Actions (examples)
These are the “social / exploration / recreation” menu.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Work the Crowd (gather rumors, find jobs, locate people)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Call in a Favor (use an existing contact/debt)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Make a New Contact (turn a face into a name you can return to)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Handle Local Business (sell cargo, buy gear, hire help, forge papers)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Stir Trouble (intimidate, provoke, leverage, start a conflict on purpose)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Lay Low (reduce Heat locally, hide the ship/crew, lose pursuers)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Indulge (recreation: drink, fight pits, VR dens, temples, clubs, shoreline, etc.)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Recover (medical care, detox, radiation treatment—if available)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Go Looking (explore the station/colony, find what’s really going on)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Do a Side Job (short task for cash/parts/data; often comes with strings)

________________


11.3 Recreation and Indulgence
When you Indulge, say what you do and what you’re trying to get out of it:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * escape
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * connection
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * pleasure
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * status
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * forgetting
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * information-by-osmosis

Then roll 2d6 +FACE (or +GRIT if it’s about pushing your limits).
                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * 10+: Choose 2.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * 7–9: Choose 1, and the GM chooses 1 consequence.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * 6-: You still get what you wanted in the moment—then the bill comes due.

Choose:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Clear a Condition (or reduce RADS by 1 if treatment exists here)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Gain +1 DATA (you overhear something useful)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Reduce HEAT by 1 (you disappear into the crowd)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Strengthen a relationship (create or upgrade a Contact)

Possible consequences:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * You owe someone (a debt, a favor, a promise)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Heat rises (you’re noticed, recorded, followed)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * You lose time (Corridor window tightens; advance Instability)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * You wake up Compromised (missing gear, blackmail, contamination scare)

11.4 Core Location Moves
Use these when Location Actions become risky.
WORK THE CROWD — roll +FACE
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * 10+: Ask 2. Gain +1 DATA.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * 7–9: Ask 1. Choose 1: pay a cost, owe a favor, or take +1 HEAT.
Questions:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Who’s really in control here?
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * What’s scarce right now?
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * What changed recently (that outsiders don’t know)?
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * What’s the safest route / worst route?
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Who’s desperate enough to deal?

MAKE A CONTACT — roll +FACE
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * 10+: They’re solid; pick 2 perks.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * 7–9: Pick 1 perk; they come with a complication.
Perks:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * They can get Parts
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * They can get Papers
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * They can get Work
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * They can hide you / reduce Heat
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * They know Corridor gossip (Data)
Complications:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * They’re hunted, bought, or lying
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * They want exclusivity
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * They’ll sell you out under pressure

GO LOOKING — roll +WITS
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * 10+: Find what you need and it’s usable.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * 7–9: Find it, but it’s dangerous / incomplete / costs time.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * 6-: You find something else—something that finds you.

11.5 Dock Phase (refit / recovery / projects)
Dock Phase is the “maintenance layer.” It may happen at the same location, but it’s a different focus.
In Dock Phase:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Each PC gets 1 Dock Action
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * The crew collectively gets 2 Ship Actions

Dock Actions (PC)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Treat & Detox (clear Conditions / reduce RADS if possible)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Acquire Gear (get tagged gear; on a miss it’s illegal/damaged/owed)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Work a Project (advance a personal project clock)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Reduce Heat (paperwork, bribes, disappearing, false IDs)

Ship Actions (Crew)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Repair a system (spend PARTS)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Refit / install a Module (project clock or cost)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Restock (convert cargo/credits into SUPPLY/FUEL/PARTS)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Decontaminate (remove contamination risks, reduce future RADS on runs)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Scrub the Record (reduce Heat, if you have access or leverage)

Note: Dock Phase is where the ship sheet changes. Location Phase is where your relationships and problems change.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * new playbook move
