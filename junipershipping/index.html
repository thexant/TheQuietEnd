Services

Freight

Crates and pallets for shops and habitats. Clear labeling and seal integrity required.

Cold-Chain

Temperature-sensitive goods (food, reagents, meds). Validated containers only.
  • Sealed Medical available for clinics and authorized customers.
  • Desk Hold for recipients without reliable local delivery access.
  • Returns supported when lanes and windows permit.
How it works

1) Drop off

Bring your parcel to the Juniper concourse desk. We verify label and seal and issue a receipt slip.

2) Transfer

Shipments move on posted Gate windows and available carrier capacity. Some routes may be quiet between windows.

3) Pickup

Recipient collects from desk hold or lockers (where supported). A receipt slip is your proof of pickup.
We keep our public info simple because it should be. If you need detailed chain-of-custody or special handling, we will tell you what paperwork to bring to the desk.

FAQ

Do you deliver door-to-door?

Where local carriers exist, we can hand off to them. Otherwise we use desk hold and lockers.

What do I need for pickup?

Your receipt slip (or recipient token, where supported) and basic ID check at the concourse desk.

Why do ETAs change?

Gate windows vary and handling load changes. We cannot guarantee arrival times will be exact.
