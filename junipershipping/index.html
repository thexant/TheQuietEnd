How it works

1) Drop off

Bring your parcel to the Juniper concourse desk. We verify label + seal and issue a manifest stub.

2) Transfer

Shipments move on posted Gate windows and available carrier capacity. Some routes may be quiet between windows.

3) Pickup

Recipient collects from desk hold or lockers (where supported). A manifest stub is your receipt.
We keep our public info simple because it should be. If you need detailed chain-of-custody or special handling, we’ll tell you what paperwork to bring to the desk.

FAQ

Do you deliver door-to-door?

Where local carriers exist, we can hand off to them. Otherwise we use desk hold and lockers.

What do I need for pickup?

Your manifest stub (or recipient token, where supported) and basic ID check at the concourse desk.

Why do ETAs change?

Gate windows vary and handling load changes. We cannot gurauntee arrival times will be accurate.
