Help // Operator Quickstart
Local reference only
1. What You're Looking At
Your Role
You manage a gate; a transit hub where ships travel between distant locations. Ships arrive constantly requesting passage. Your job is to process them safely while keeping gate systems operational.
What is a Gate?
A gate is a transit hub built to stabilize corridor routes and connect locations throughout the galaxy. Ships use gates to jump between distant star systems that would otherwise be unreachable. Your gate has multiple systems (power, docking, decontamination, communications) that degrade over time and must be maintained.
Success
You earn credits by processing ships and surviving your shift. One critical mistake: approving a hazardous ship, running out of fuel, losing all relays — will SEVER your gate connection. GAME OVER. Your shift officially ends at 8 hours, but you can continue indefinitely if you stay vigilant.
What is Severing?
When your gate loses power, communications fail completely, or a catastrophic event occurs, the gate SEVERS from the network. This ends your session. All severing conditions are preventable if you maintain systems and check ships carefully.
2. Your Interface
Top Bar
Shows your operator callsign (editable), gate ID, current credits earned, shift time, and alert count. The right side has controls for theme, sound, visual effects, HELP, and RESET (danger — wipes your session).
TRAFFIC Panel
Two queues: INBOUND (ships arriving at your gate) and OUTBOUND (ships departing from your gate). Each ship shows ID, priority, type, cargo, status, and action buttons (DTL, QRY, DECON, APP, DENY). Ships with CRITICAL priority have strict deadlines — missing them can SEVER your gate.
SYSTEMS Panel
Monitors your gate's health across 7 subsections: Gate Systems (beacon, alignment, sync), Corridor Systems (transit links to other gates), Facility Systems (decon bay, fuel manifold, dock clamps), Resources (fuel and supply levels), Concourse (current ship population), Relays (communication network), and Maintenance (active work orders). Systems show ONLINE (green), DEGRADED (yellow), or OFFLINE (red).
TERMINAL Panel
Bottom section with three parts: Console Log (command output), Events Log (automatic notifications), and Command Input (where you type commands like STATUS or APPROVE V-9012). Four maintenance buttons sit above the input: REALIGN, RESET, PURGE, SCAN.
3. What You Do First
First 2 Minutes
  1. Ships will appear in TRAFFIC immediately. Check the INBOUND queue first.
  2. Click DTL (Detail) on the first ship to see full information: cargo, transponder status, effects/hazards.
  3. Look for warnings in red text or hazard flags like "CORRIDOR RADIATION", "VACUUM BLOOM", "RAIDERS", "PIRATES".
  4. If the ship is clean (no hazards, valid transponder), click APP (Approve). If hazardous, click DENY and select the appropriate reason.
  5. Glance at SYSTEMS panel. If anything shows DEGRADED or FAULT, you'll need to address it soon.
Basic Loop
Process ships as they arrive, monitor system health, acknowledge relay pings, order fuel when low, repair degraded facilities. The game never pauses. Systems degrade randomly. Ships keep coming. Stay active.
4. Ship Management
APPROVE (APP)
Clears a ship for transit through your gate. You earn +4 credits. Ship enters the Concourse (increases population). WARNING: Approving an INBOUND ship with VACUUM BLOOM, RAIDERS, or PIRATES will INSTANTLY SEVER YOUR GATE and end the game.
DENY
Refuses ship entry/exit. Correct denials earn +3 credits. Incorrect denials (denying a safe ship without valid cause) cost -2 credits. When denying, you must provide a reason like "QUARANTINE REQUIRED" or "AUTHORITIES NOTIFIED". Denying won't end your game, but approving the wrong ship will.
HOLD
Pauses a ship for 30-60 seconds. Use this when you need time to investigate, run SCAN, or QUERY a ship. Each ship can only be held ONCE. Use it wisely.
QUERY (QRY)
Requests ship identification. Use on ships showing UNKNOWN or MISMATCH transponder status. Ship will respond after a delay. Some ships won't respond or will respond with false information — use SCAN to detect deception.
DECON
Starts decontamination procedure. Ships with CORRIDOR RADIATION must be decontaminated before approval (inbound) or must be denied (outbound — you cannot decon outbound ships safely). Requires DECON BAY facility to be ONLINE. Costs supply.
DETAIL (DTL)
Shows complete ship information: ID, state, cargo, transponder status, priority level, effects/hazards, notes, ETA, dock slot. ALWAYS CHECK DETAIL BEFORE APPROVING. One missed hazard = instant game over.
Ship Hazards Reference
Hazard Where Correct Action If Wrong
Vacuum Bloom Inbound DENY: QUARANTINE REQUIRED GAME OVER
Corridor Radiation Inbound DECON, then APPROVE Contamination risk
Corridor Radiation Outbound DENY: RADIATION HAZARD GAME OVER
Raiders / Pirates Any DENY: AUTHORITIES NOTIFIED GAME OVER
MISMATCH transponder Any QUERY first, then decide Risk of hidden threat
UNKNOWN transponder Any QUERY + SCAN recommended Risk of hidden threat
Hidden threats Any Use SCAN to detect Approve unknowingly = disaster
Outbound Deadlines
Outbound ships have departure deadlines (countdown timers). CRITICAL priority ships must be processed quickly - missing their deadline will SEVER your gate. URGENT ships cause credit penalties if delayed. STANDARD ships are lower risk but still cost credits if you miss their window.
5. Systems Maintenance
Beacon
Status: ONLINE / FAULT / OFFLINE. Your gate's visibility signal to the network. If OFFLINE, other gates can't see you and fewer ships will arrive. No direct fix — use RESET command which can sometimes restore it. Degrades randomly.
Alignment
Status: WITHIN TOLERANCE / DRIFTING / OUT OF TOLERANCE. Gate positioning accuracy for safe transit. If OUT OF TOLERANCE, ships cannot transit safely. Fix: Type REALIGN command or click REALIGN button (15-second cooldown). Use preventively when showing DRIFTING.
Sync
Status: SYNC / ASYNC / FAULT. Network synchronization state. If FAULT, gate connection becomes unstable and may lead to severing. Fix: Type RESET command or click RESET button (15-second cooldown). Healthy relays also help maintain sync.
Corridors (Links)
Status: STABLE / DEGRADED / INTERMITTENT / OFFLINE. Transit paths connecting your gate to others (usually 3-4 links labeled A, B, C, D). Like highways between cities. If ALL corridors go OFFLINE, your gate loses network connection and SEVERS (GAME OVER). No direct fix — corridors degrade and recover randomly. Maintain overall gate health to reduce risk.
Facilities
Status: ONLINE / DEGRADED / OFFLINE. Gate station equipment (DECON BAY for decontamination, FUEL MANIFOLD for refueling, DOCK CLAMP ARRAY for docking, BEACON AMPLIFIER for signal strength, etc.). WARNING: DEGRADED facilities automatically fail to OFFLINE after 2-5 minutes if not repaired. If ALL facilities are OFFLINE with no active work orders, gate SEVERS (GAME OVER). Different facilities being offline will have different impacts on gameplay.
Repairing Facilities
Option 1 (Quick): Type REPAIR [system name] or click REPAIR button. 70% success rate, instant if successful, 5-second cooldown. Option 2 (Guaranteed): Click WO (Work Order) button. 100% success, takes 1-3 minutes, costs supply. Issue work orders on critical facilities to guarantee repair.
Relays
Status: ONLINE / WEAK / FAULT / OFFLINE. Communication stations (2-3 per gate, labeled R-XXXX) that maintain your connection to the network. Relays periodically send PING requests. You MUST click ACK or type ACK [relay-id] within 10 seconds of a relay ping. Unacknowledged pings degrade relay health. If ALL relays go OFFLINE for 10+ seconds, gate SEVERS (GAME OVER). Enable audio to hear ping alerts.
Relay Commands
ACK [relay-id]: Acknowledge relay ping (CRITICAL! Do this ASAP!). PING [relay-id]: Test relay connection manually. RESTORE [relay-id]: Attempt to repair FAULT/WEAK relay (risky, can backfire). Click ACK button on relay when it shows pending ping.
FUEL
Drains continuously, when it reaches 25% or less it will show CRITICAL warning. At 0% your gate loses power and SEVERS (GAME OVER). Fix: Click ORDER buttons under Resources or type ORDER FUEL [amount] (e.g., ORDER FUEL 50). Costs credits, takes time to deliver. Order fuel at 30-40%, not when critical.
SUPPLY
Used for work orders and decontamination operations. Doesn't drain passively. Running out means you cannot issue work orders or decon ships. Fix: Click ORDER buttons or type ORDER SUPPLY [amount]. Order supply when below 30% if you have active maintenance needs.
Concourse
Shows current ship population at your gate (6-26+). Increases when you approve ships, decreases over time. High concourse levels (overcrowding) can cause issues. Fix: Type PURGE command or click PURGE button to clear low-priority traffic (costs 10 FUEL, 15-second cooldown).

7. How You Lose (SEVERING)
1. FUEL DEPLETION
FUEL reaches 0% → Power loss → SEVERED. Why: You didn't order fuel in time or ran out of credits. Prevention: Order fuel when below 30%, keep credits available, monitor fuel constantly.
2. RELAY FAILURE
All relays offline for 10+ seconds → Network disconnected → SEVERED. Why: You ignored relay PING requests or didn't ACK them within 10 seconds. Prevention: ACK relay pings IMMEDIATELY when they appear. Enable audio to hear ping alerts.
3. CORRIDOR COLLAPSE
All corridor links offline → Lost network connection → SEVERED. Why: Random degradation, no direct fix available. Prevention: Maintain overall gate health, keep systems stable to reduce corridor degradation risk.
4. FACILITY FAILURE
All facilities offline with no active work orders → System failure → SEVERED. Why: You let facilities degrade from DEGRADED to OFFLINE (happens in 2-5 min) without repairing them. Prevention: Issue work orders or use REPAIR command when facilities show DEGRADED.
5. VACUUM BLOOM
Approved inbound ship with Vacuum Bloom reaches gate → Outbreak → INSTANT SEVERED. Why: You approved a hazardous ship without checking DETAIL. Prevention: ALWAYS check ship DETAIL before approving. DENY any ship with Vacuum Bloom using reason "QUARANTINE REQUIRED".
6. RAIDERS/PIRATES
Approved ship with Raiders or Pirates reaches gate → Attack → INSTANT SEVERED. Why: You approved hostile ships. Prevention: Use SCAN command regularly (every 60-90s) to detect hidden threats. DENY with reason "AUTHORITIES NOTIFIED" when detected.
7. RADIATION EXPOSURE
Approved outbound ship with Corridor Radiation departs → Gate exposure → INSTANT SEVERED. Why: You let a contaminated ship depart from your gate. Prevention: DECON inbound ships with radiation before approval. DENY any outbound ship showing radiation with reason "RADIATION HAZARD".
8. CRITICAL DEADLINE
Failed to process CRITICAL priority ship (emergency TOW request) on time → Ship lost → INSTANT SEVERED. Why: You didn't prioritize high-urgency ships. Prevention: Process CRITICAL priority ships first, watch countdown timers, use HOLD if you need time to verify safety.
9. ENTERING DEBT
Reaching 0 (or less) total wage after an action that costs CR (incorrect approvals/denies, spending on resources, etc) → INSTANT SEVERED. Why: You didn't manage your incoming wage versus spending or made too many small mistakes that added up to cost you in the end. Keep an eye on WAGE and be careful!
8. Tips for Survival
Critical Habits
  • Check DETAIL on EVERY ship before approving. One hazard missed = instant game over.
  • Use SCAN command every 60-90 seconds to detect hidden threats on ships.
  • ACK relay pings IMMEDIATELY when they appear. Set audio ON to hear alerts.
  • Order FUEL when below 40%, not when critical. Fuel takes time to deliver.
  • Repair facilities at DEGRADED status. Don't wait for OFFLINE (harder to fix).
  • Watch outbound ship deadlines. CRITICAL priority ships can SEVER your gate if missed.
  • When in doubt, use HOLD to pause a ship, then SCAN and DETAIL to investigate.
  • Incorrect DENYs cost credits but won't end your game. Incorrect APPROVEs will.
  • Use REALIGN and RESET preventively (15s cooldowns). PURGE costs 10 FUEL.
  • You earn 0.25 credits per minute + credits per action.
Save/Load/Share
SHARE copies a URL snapshot of your session. EXPORT downloads a .tqegate save file. LOAD FILE restores a saved session. Use these to preserve long successful shifts.
Interface Settings
THEME changes colors (12 options). SFX/MUSIC toggle audio (recommend ON for relay ping alerts). VFX toggles visual effects (use LOW on low performance devices). RESET button (top right) wipes your session completely.
