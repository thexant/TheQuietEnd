# The Ancestral War

While the term **“The Ancestral War”** was not used to describe the conflict until some time after it had ended, it describes the reasons for the war quite clearly. The Ancestral War was a conflict fought between the Confederation of Earth (The Confederation) and the newly established Children of Earth (COE) spanning the years 2482 until 2495 over the legitimacy of the Confederations power and restriction of access to Sol and by extension, Earth.

## The First Skirmishes

The Ancestral War first broke out in early (roughly March) 2482 when a fleet aligned with the Children of Earth was stopped by a Confederation Fleet and declared to be “under arrest” for behaviours with intention to undermine the Confederation. In response, the COE fleet fired on the unprepared Confederation fleet using illegally constructed hardpoints - the Confederation fleet was destroyed and the COE fleet able to withdraw before reinforcements arrived. Later that year, (July) the Confederation “responded” to the earlier attack by surrounding *Axle Orbital*, a station serving as an operations hub for COE loyalists. The loyalists were ordered to surrender but instead sent out a small combat fleet that they had been preparing. The resulting skirmish ended with Axle Orbital being destroyed along with the majority of both the Confederation fleet and entire COE fleet deployed from the Station. Approximately 360 were killed, and this marked the proper beginning of the war.

## The War Heats Up

By late 2482 and Early 2483 both the Confederation and Children of Earth were heavily arming themselves and preparing - small skirmishes occurred in systems along the “front lines” and nearby but no major conflicts had occurred since the attack on Axle Orbital. In Early 2483 the COE entered *Pollard’s System,* a fairly low population system with 2 corridor connections to high value Confederation systems and one other connection, via GATE-2344 which connected to an uninhabited system ( *CS-0183 )* which itself connected to the *Triple Stream* system; an operations hub for the COE. Upon entering *Pollard’s System* the COE took control of GATE-2344 (which had been operated by Confederation employed Gate Operators) and began to use the system as a staging ground for an attack on the connected Confederation Systems, *Tavuld* and *Steel’s Will*. Fortunately for the Confederation, a small privately owned ship had seen the attack on GATE-2344 and managed to bring news to the Confederation via the *Tavuld* system. The Confederation responded by sending fleets into *Pollard’s System* resulting in the first large conflict of the war. Fighting lasted approximately 13 days - with both sides regularly reinforcing lost ships through their respective corridors. In the end, the COE was being weakened and its nearby infrastructure unable to support continued reinforcement so in a desperate withdrawal destroyed GATE-2344 after most of its surviving ships had retreated, thus denying the confederation access into *CS-0183* and further into COE territory with remaining ships holding off the Confederation in order to make this withdrawal possible. Following the retreat from Pollard’s System and the destruction of GATE-2344, the Confederation attempted to follow the COE back to their operations hub in the *Triple Stream* system. However, without a proper gate connection, the Confederation was forced to attempt "blind-jumps" through ungated openings and accelerate its attempts to locate new corridor routes that could connect to the system. Over two years, dozens of Confederation ships were lost to Corridor Radiation or were later discovered destroyed and/or derelict due to the lack of gate stabilization. Realizing they could not safely invade, the Confederation transitioned from direct combat to a blockade. They successfully intercepted supply ships carrying food, oxygen and other critical supplies to COE aligned systems, effectively starving out the Children of Earth.

## New Tactics

In Mid 2485 an explosion occurred at a Confederation Colony, *Lightstone,* causing a catastrophic depressurization event and killing every colonist nearly immediately. Shortly after, a massive COE fleet entered the system, *Erandal,* and began to push through further into Confederation owned systems - striking infrastructure and military installations but at times also striking larger civilian settlements. The Confederation responded both militarily with its own fleets at first, causing large scale fighting to break out across several systems. The Confederation then began to respond by firing on COE civilian settlements and disabling or destroying gates connecting to COE territory - effectively causing entire systems to die slow deaths in isolation for the years to follow. The initial fighting was intense and visceral but continued for months, which eventually became years. As time passed, direct ship to ship combat lessened but guerrilla attacks on both sides continued to occur. This caused many to view to the COE as extremists, while those loyal to them or sharing a common dissatisfaction with the Confederation to believe they were doing what they had to in order to stop an omnipresent bureaucracy that meant nothing to them; but on the contrary also caused many to view the Confederation as brutal, fascistic and cruel to its enemies. As the war progressed, both sides realized that information was the most valuable resource. Since FTL radio was basic, unreliable, and easily lost due to Exit-Point Misalignment, strategy depended entirely on physical data and information carried by ships. The COE launched the "Courier-Kill" campaign, specifically targeting small, fast Confederation vessels moving between systems. By destroying these couriers, the COE created information gaps where Confederation Commanders and Admirals were forced to make major decisions without hearing from Earth for months at a time.

## Dying Down and the Ends

After 10 years of continued conflicts, skirmishes and guerilla strikes from both sides the war began to die down significantly. The Confederation, now weakened and viewed by many as an equal evil, had managed to push the COE back to one system and nearly eradicated its military forces. While Raul Friedan was known to be the founder of the COE movement, by the end of the war his position as Chairman of the COE had been replaced by Elizabeth Donelly \- a feverishly loyal and patriotic dedicated member of the COE cause. The Confederation managed to surround *Respite Port*, the colony in which the COE had been reduced to operating from and where Elizabeth Donelly was living and ordered the remaining members to surrender. As the initial members of the COE began to surrender, coming into the docking bay in small groups or one at a time with their hands raised, an explosion shook the colony. Plumes of fire filled the halls and reached as far as the docking bay. Fortunately, the breached area was minimal and the colony recoverable, but Elizabeth had chosen the alternative to surrender and with her died the true faction of the Children of Earth, ending the war.

## Weapons and Tools of War

Because the Ancestral War was a mix of guerilla warfare and ship to ship combat \- the type of weapons used were often designed to maximize their impact. The most common type of weapon were APHE rockets equipped with high speed propellants, designed to pierce the hull of an enemy ship or fortification before exploding and causing catastrophic damage. Other commonly used hardpoints consisted of high velocity chainguns, designed for shredding through light armor and vital ship components, railguns for piercing hulls and causing significant irreparable damage. Though hand to hand or close quarters combat was minimal, instances of basic assault rifles and handguns being used did occur, often these were rudimentary, bulky and built for necessity rather than mass produced. Other weapons included basic bombs and explosives as well as repurposed industrial equipment during times of desperation. In the later years, the COE used modified mining railguns and industrial plasma cutters originally meant for asteroid processing to defend their stations from Confederation attacks.
